About This Site

Japan Travel Guide is a destination resource covering all 47 prefectures of Japan. Whether you are planning your first trip or looking to explore beyond the well-known cities, this site helps you discover the best each region has to offer — from Hokkaido in the north to Okinawa in the south.

Click any prefecture on the interactive map to see its Top 5 recommended spots. Every No.1 ranked destination includes a detailed article on attractions, local food, getting there, and the best souvenirs to bring home.

Our Goal

We aim to provide honest, well-researched travel information for every corner of Japan — not just the famous cities. Each prefecture has unique food, landscapes, history, and crafts worth discovering, and this guide exists to make that easy to find.

All content is reviewed regularly to keep recommendations accurate and up to date.
